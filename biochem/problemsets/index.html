Sample Exercises

Select an exercise below. The guided exercises offer step-by-step instructions showing you how to use StarBiochem. The un-guided exercises contain only biology information and questions. A complete guide for navigating StarBiochem can be found in our manual.

DNA Glycosylase

  • Explore the relationship between a protein’s structure and its function in a human DNA glycosylase called human 8-oxoguanine glycosylase (hOGG1). Part 1 is appropiate for advanced high school biology courses and introductory undergraduate biology courses. Part 2 is suitable for advance undergraduate biology courses.
guided exercise

Hemoglobin & Sickle Cell Anemia

  • Explore how a single alteration in the amino acid sequence of the hemoglobin protein results in sickle cell anemia, a genetically-inherited blood disorder. Appropriate for high school biology courses and introductory undergraduate biology courses.
guided exercise

Macromolecules

  • This exercise explores the four classes of biological macromolecules. Appropriate for high school biology courses.
guided exercise

Phenylalanine Hydroxylase & PKU Disorder

  • Explore the relationship between the structure of a mutated form of PAH and the function of this mutated protein in patients with phenylketonuria (PKU), a genetically inherited disorder that is characterized by the inability to utilize the essential amino acid, phenylalanine.ppropriate for introductory undergraduate biology courses.
un-guided exercise

Phosphofructokinase (PFK)

  • Explore the structure and function of the enzyme phosphofructokinase (PFK), the most important regularoty enzyme of glycolysis. Appropriate for introductory undergraduate biology courses.
un-guided exercise

Ras and Cell Signaling

  • Ras proteins are essential components of various signal transduction pathways that regulate growth, proliferation, differentiation, and apoptosis. In this exercise you will explore the structure of the Ras protein in its active and inactive states and the amino acids that contribute to these two states. Appropriate for introductory undergraduate biology courses.
un-guided exercise
