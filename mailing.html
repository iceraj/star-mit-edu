Thank you for visiting the STAR website!

The STAR program at MIT seeks to bridge the divide between scientific research and the classroom by developing innovative and intuitive teaching tools for classroom use. Our tools and curriculum materials are FREELY available on our website.

STAR Biology software tools

StarBiochem is a protein 3-D viewer designed with students in mind, that allows for the visualization and manipulation of any Protein Data Bank (PDB) structure.

StarGenetics is a genetics cross simulator, where experiments provided to students can be easily customized by an instructor.
