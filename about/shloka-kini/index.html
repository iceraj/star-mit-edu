Shloka Kini

Shloka Kini worked as a STAR software developer in MIT’s Office of Educational Innovation and Technology. Her archived OEIT profile identifies StarCellBio as the focus of her web development work.

StarCellBio was an NSF-funded project to let students practice designing and interpreting experiments in cell and molecular biology. Kini’s contribution formed part of the STAR team’s effort to make those research methods accessible in teaching.
