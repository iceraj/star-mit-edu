Also, you'll need to modify line 19 of /etc/init.d/ec2-get-credentials to:

until(new IO::Socket::INET("<ip-address>:8773")){print"Waiting for network...
\n";sleep 1}

Replace <ip-address> with the cloud controller's ip.

~Justin
