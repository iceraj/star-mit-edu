I don't think StarCluster plays with SELINUX at all. Is this a custom AMI of your own? Disabling selinux depends on the OS a bit I think? What OS?

I've found two places to do this in CentOS 7:

/etc/selinux/config
/etc/sysconfig/selinux

Not sure which overrides the other, but making sure they're both ' SELINUX=disabled' seems to do the trick.

-Hugh
