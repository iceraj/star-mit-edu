Hi All,

Just wanted to announce that I recently migrated the latest x86/x86_64
StarCluster 9.10 AMIs to the eu-west-1 region. If those of you in the EU
region could test these for me that'd be great.

Just for reference, to switch regions from us-east-1 (default) to
eu-west-1 open your ~/.starcluster/config and add the following settings
to your [aws info] section:

---------------------------------------------
[aws info]
....
aws_region_name=eu-west-1
aws_region_host=ec2.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com
---------------------------------------------

After that you should be able to see the images with:

-----------------------------------------------------------------
$ starcluster listpublic
StarCluster - (http://web.mit.edu/starcluster) (v. 0.9999)
Software Tools for Academics and Researchers (STAR)
Please submit bug reports to starcluster_at_mit.edu

--------------

total images: 2
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Now that I have the migration process down I'll do the same for
us-west-1 and ap-southeast-1. I'll announce those as they come.

~Justin
