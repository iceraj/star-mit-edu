On Fri, Jan 16, 2015 at 4:01 PM, Jennifer Staab <jstaab_at_cs.unc.edu> wrote:

> I can't attest to the behavior of StarCluster regarding AMI creation
> because I usually do my AMI creation from AWS console. I can say that in
> the past I had SSH go down if I created an AMI of a running instance.
> Luckily after the AMI was completed and I reboot a few times I got SSH
> functionality back without having to mount my EBS backed instance to
> another running instance to restore SSH functionality. After that
> experience I just stop the instance prior to making an AMI. This of course
> only works on EBS backed instances where the stopping them doesn't result
> in termination -- also stopping will dump all that you have stored in
> instance storage. If rebooting the instance doesn't work you can look
> here <http://alestic.com/2011/02/ec2-fix-ebs-root> for directions on
> mounting to another running instance to restore functionality.
>
> Good Luck!
> -Jennifer
