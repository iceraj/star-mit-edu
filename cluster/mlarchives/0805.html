> > > > > Grid Engine assumes by default a  1:1

> > > parallel
> > > > environment.

> > > > queues up and
> > > > > runs more than one of your threaded app

> > > the

> > the
> > > > detriment of all.
> > > > >
> > > > > One way around that would be to

> > > exclusive
> > > > job access and
> > > > > submit your job with the "exclusive"

> > > > > your job when it runs will get an

> > > execution

> > > > >
> > > > > Another way is to fake up a parallel

> > > For

> > parallel
> > > > environment called

> > > apps

> > > > > oversubscribing an execution host.
