Fellow Starcluster and other HPC tech users,

I am hoping Amazon will allow EFS within the new regions very soon. EFS
will be critical for cost savings with some of the customers I support.
Indeed we are using Starcluster with some changes of our own to support
multiple EBS volumes.

I also voice my concern for the Starcluster project as a whole given its
decentralized feature and bug improvement. It would be great if MIT took
the reigns again to bring the latest and greatest user improvements into
one coalesced v1.0.

Just adding my voice to the choir as they say.

Eduardo
