Hi Kwon,
Welcome to Starcluster.

100 EC2 instances seems excessive. The High performance instances are
not cheap either. Most people start with 3-5 instances. I have no idea
what your job profile looks like, but you can add nodes to a running
cluster with the add_node command if you need more. You probably want
to experiment with a small job first and then scale up.


Good luck,
Rajat
