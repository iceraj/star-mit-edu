Hi Guys,
     I just wanted to announce that earlier this week I open sourced
what is essentially a StarCluster web interface. I have been kicking
around a commercial service, Crunch.io, that utilizes StarCluster, for
the past few months. For the most part, this dashboard it what I have
accomplished. Its pretty raw right now, but will allow you to create,
launch and terminate clusters in EC2. Here is my blog post announcing it:
     http://crunch.io/2010/09/07/open-source-crunch-dashboard.html

Any feedback is appreciated, though I do have a todo list about a mile
long. Chances are I have thought of anything that seems obvious, though
feedback will help me prioritize issues.
