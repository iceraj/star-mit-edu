Sergio,
I looked at the code that is causing your problems. It's this line:
return datetime.datetime.strptime(str, "%a %b %d %H:%M:%S UTC %Y")

where 'str' is the output of the *remote* call to date. My mac returns this:
rbanerjee:~/starcluster/StarCluster/starcluster/balancers/sge $ date
Tue Apr 30 13:12:44 EDT 2013
Which looks like it would time format OK. One oddity is that your time format is returning PDT when UTC is expected:

Do you know why your time zone may be set differently than other AWS instances we've used? Custom images?
