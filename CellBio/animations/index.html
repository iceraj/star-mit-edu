Video Animations

StarCellBio contains 7 animations on each of the experimental techniques contained within StarCellBio as well as relevant cell and molecular biology concepts. All of the animations are available below as well as from the 'Videos' link at the top of each page within StarCellBioand on our StarCellBio YouTube Channel.

The animations were developed in collaboration with Janet Iwasa, Research Assistant Professor at the University of Utah. Please see each animation's credits for more information on all of the contributors. The animations are all available under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International license.

Western Blotting - Part I

The first western blotting animation in a series of three animations will cover protein isolation.

Western Blotting - Part II

The second animation in a series of three animations on western blotting will cover polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis.

Western Blotting - Part III

The last animation in a series of three animations on western blotting will cover transfer, blotting and visualization.

Flow Cytometry

This animation on flow cytometry will introduce you to the flow cytometry experimental technique.

Basic Microscopy

In this animation, you will be introduced to the microscopy experimental technique and a basic light microscope.

Fluoresence Microscopy

In this animation, you will be introduced to fluorescence microscopy, which is a specialized type of light microscopy.

Fluorescence

This animation will introduce you to the concept of fluorescence and the reasons why fluorescence-based techniques are used in cell and molecular biology experiments.
